# Baggage & Allowance Policy (Doc ID: POL-BAG-002)

## 1. Cabin Baggage Allowance
- **Economy Class**: 1 cabin bag (max 7kg, 55 x 38 x 20 cm) + 1 small personal item (laptop bag or purse).
- **Business Class**: 2 cabin pieces (total combined weight up to 14kg).
- **First Class**: 2 cabin pieces (total combined weight up to 16kg).

## 2. Checked Baggage Allowance
- **Basic Economy**: 1 checked bag up to 20kg.
- **Flexible Economy**: 2 checked bags up to 23kg each (total 46kg).
- **Business Class**: 2 checked bags up to 32kg each (total 64kg).
- **First Class**: 3 checked bags up to 32kg each (total 96kg).

## 3. Excess Baggage Fees
- Excess weight between 23kg and 32kg is charged at $50 per bag.
- Additional separate bags beyond the allowed allowance are charged at $100 per piece for short-haul and $175 per piece for long-haul routes.
