# Airline Cancellation & Refund Policy (Doc ID: POL-REFUND-001)

## 1. Fare-Class Rules
- **Basic Economy Fare**:
  - Non-refundable. No monetary refund is permitted upon customer voluntary cancellation.
  - Government and airport taxes are refunded upon request minus an administrative processing fee ($50).
  - No flight changes or itinerary date modifications are allowed.
- **Flexible Fare**:
  - Fully refundable up to 24 hours prior to scheduled departure time with a nominal 10% cancellation fee.
  - Cancellations made within 24 hours of departure are subject to a 25% cancellation fee.
  - Full value can be converted into an active Travel Credit voucher with 12-month validity and zero cancellation penalty.

## 2. 24-Hour Cooling-Off Rule
- Any booking made at least 7 days before departure can be cancelled within 24 hours of purchase for a 100% full cash refund to the original payment method with zero penalty, regardless of fare type.

## 3. Airline-Initiated Cancellations
- If a flight is cancelled by the airline due to technical, weather, or operational disruptions:
  - Passengers are entitled to either free rebooking on the next available flight or an immediate 100% full refund with no cancellation fees.
  - If cancellation occurs within 3 hours of departure, additional hotel accommodation and meal vouchers are provided.
