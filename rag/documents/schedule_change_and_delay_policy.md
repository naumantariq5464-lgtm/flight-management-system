# Schedule Change, Delays & Compensation Policy (Doc ID: POL-SCHED-003)

## 1. Schedule Modifications by Airline
- When a flight departure or arrival is modified by more than 2 hours:
  - Passengers receive free rebooking on any alternative flight within ±7 days.
  - Passengers who reject the schedule change are eligible for a 100% full refund with zero deductions.

## 2. Flight Delays & Duty of Care
- **Delays > 2 Hours**: Complimentary refreshments, phone calls, and internet access.
- **Delays > 4 Hours**: Free warm meal vouchers.
- **Overnight Delays**: Complimentary 4-star hotel accommodation with airport shuttle transfers.

## 3. Denied Boarding / Overbooking Compensation
- In cases of controlled overbooking where involuntary denied boarding occurs:
  - Passengers are rebooked on the next fastest available connection.
  - Automatic cash compensation of $400 (flights under 1500km) or $800 (flights over 3500km) is issued following supervisor sign-off.
